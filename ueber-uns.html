About SimLab

SimLab – Students for Surgery

Who we are

SimLab – Students for Surgery is a student-led initiative at the Universitätsklinikum Hamburg-Eppendorf, founded in April 2023.

Our Vision

SimLab aims to give medical students a practical introduction to surgery. Our main focus is on basic laparoscopic skills: in training sessions on simulators, students learn and practise fundamental techniques such as camera handling, instrument coordination, and cutting and suturing under laparoscopic view.

For students who are also interested in the technical side, we additionally offer an introduction to 3D printing and CAD design. Participants learn how anatomical and surgical training models are designed and produced, and can help develop models for our own training sessions.

All of our courses are organised on a voluntary basis and offered as an extracurricular programme. They are open to all interested students, regardless of prior experience or semester.

Our Goals

Teaching

Making basic laparoscopic skills accessible to every interested student

Innovation

Developing our own 3D-printed training models for surgical skills

Networking

Gathering and linking people interested in surgery and 3D printing
